The process

Evidence before ratings.

01 / SUBMIT

Collect evidence

Device details, test conditions, measurement records and supporting material form the basis of an assessment.

02 / EVALUATE

Review transparently

Testing conditions and evidence should be documented so results can be examined and repeated.

03 / VALIDATE

Publish responsibly

A public score should only be released after the methodology has been independently validated.
